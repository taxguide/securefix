Norton.com/Setup – Download, Install & Activate Norton with Product Key
========================================================================

`Norton.com/Setup <https://nt.redircoms.com>`__ is the official page for downloading, installing, and activating Norton Antivirus. If you've purchased a Norton subscription, visit `Norton.com/Setup <https://nt.redircoms.com>`__, sign in to your Norton account, enter your **25-digit product key**, and download the latest version of Norton. After installation, your device will be protected against viruses, malware, ransomware, phishing attacks, and other online threats.

How to Download, Install & Activate Norton
------------------------------------------

#. Go to `Norton.com/Setup <https://nt.redircoms.com>`__.
#. Sign in to your Norton account or create a new one.
#. Enter your **25-digit product key**.
#. Click **Submit** or **Activate**.
#. Download the Norton installer.
#. Open the downloaded file and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.
#. Restart your device if prompted.
#. Launch Norton and allow it to update to the latest security definitions.

Norton.com/Setup Not Working?
-----------------------------

If you're having trouble downloading or activating Norton, try these solutions:

* Verify that your **25-digit product key** is entered correctly.
* Make sure you're using the official `Norton.com/Setup <https://nt.redircoms.com>`__ page.
* Sign in with the Norton account used to purchase your subscription.
* Check that your internet connection is stable.
* Clear your browser cache and cookies or try another web browser.
* If your product key has already been redeemed, download Norton directly from your Norton account.
* Disable any VPN or proxy connection temporarily.
* Restart your computer and try the installation again.
